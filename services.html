How to Adopt

Adopting a pet is an exciting and rewarding experience. Follow these general steps to find your new companion and provide them with a loving forever home.

  1. Research and Plan: Consider your lifestyle, living arrangements, budget, and the type of pet that would best fit your household.
  2. Find an Adoption Organization: Research local animal shelters, rescue organizations, and reputable adoption agencies.
  3. Meet Potential Pets: Visit the shelter, interact with available animals, and ask about their temperament, medical history, and care requirements.
  4. Complete an Application: Submit the adoption application and provide any requested information, references, or documentation.
  5. Prepare Your Home: Purchase necessary supplies, including food, bedding, toys, bowls, and other pet essentials. Make sure your home is pet-friendly.
  6. Finalize the Adoption: Review and sign the adoption agreement, pay applicable fees, and obtain the pet's medical and vaccination records.
  7. Welcome Your New Pet: Give your pet time to adjust, establish daily routines, and provide plenty of patience, attention, and affection.
  8. Schedule a Veterinary Visit: Arrange an initial checkup to review your pet's health, vaccinations, and preventative care needs.

Remember: Adopting a pet is a lifelong commitment. With proper preparation, patience, and love, you can give your new companion a safe and happy forever home!

Volunteer

Want to make a difference in the lives of animals? We welcome volunteers who are passionate about animal welfare and want to support our shelter and rescue efforts.

Volunteer Opportunities

  • Animal Care Assistant: Help with feeding, grooming, cleaning, and providing daily care and attention to animals.
  • Dog Walker/Socialization: Take dogs on walks, provide exercise, and help with basic socialization.
  • Cat Socialization: Spend time playing with, comforting, and socializing cats and kittens.
  • Adoption Support: Assist with adoption events, greet potential adopters, and help connect animals with their forever homes.
  • Events and Fundraising: Help organize community events, donation drives, and fundraising activities.
  • Administrative Support: Assist with answering inquiries, organizing records, updating information, and other office tasks.
  • Photography and Outreach: Take photos, create promotional materials, and help share available animals through social media and community outreach.

Volunteer Hours

Volunteer schedules are flexible and may vary depending on the role and organizational needs. Volunteers may be able to contribute a few hours per week, assist during scheduled events, or arrange recurring shifts.

Specific schedules and availability will be discussed during the volunteer application and onboarding process.

Compensation

All volunteer positions are currently unpaid. Volunteers generously donate their time and services to support animal care, adoption efforts, and community outreach.

Getting Started

Interested in volunteering? complete the volunteer form. We will contact you with questions and information about our next volunteer orientation.

How to Foster

Not ready to adopt but still want to help? Fostering a pet provides a temporary, loving home for an animal while they wait for their forever family.

  1. Learn About Fostering: Research local animal shelters and rescue organizations to understand their foster programs, requirements, and expectations.
  2. Choose a Foster Program: Find an organization that matches your availability, living situation, and experience with animals.
  3. Complete an Application: Submit a foster application and provide any requested information about your household, experience, and living arrangements.
  4. Prepare Your Home: Create a safe, comfortable space for your foster pet. Gather necessary supplies and make sure your home is pet-friendly.
  5. Welcome Your Foster Pet: Give your foster animal time to adjust, establish routines, and provide care, attention, and socialization.
  6. Support Their Journey: Work with the shelter or rescue organization to provide updates, attend appointments, and help promote your foster pet for adoption.
  7. Celebrate Their Adoption: Help your foster pet transition to their permanent home when they find their forever family!

Remember: Fostering saves lives by opening up shelter space for other animals in need. Even temporary care can make a lasting difference!

Safety First

If the animal is acting aggress, do not attempt to capture the animal yourself

Please contact your local animal control.

This goes double if they are wild animal (non-domesticated species)

Escape Artist
  • Do not chase the animal! - It will likely have an opposite desired effect
  • Sit down - Being down on the animal's level will help calm them
  • Put your hand out slowly- Palm down. If they act aggressive at this point, level and contact animal control.
  • At this point, if the animal is domesticated it will likely come to you. Do not force the animal
