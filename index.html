Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is dedicated to rescuing, protecting, and advocating for animals in need. Through compassionate care, responsible adoption, fostering, community partnerships, and education, we work to give every animal the opportunity for a healthy, safe, and loving life.

Our Vision

Our vision is a no-kill community where every animal has the opportunity to thrive. We strive to make that vision a reality through responsible pet ownership, accessible resources, community education, and a commitment to finding every animal their forever home.

How You Can Help?

  • Volunteer

    We have a number of open positions and we're waiting to hear from you!

  • Donate

    We are always looking for support. Even a few dollars can make a big difference!

    We also accept food, blankets/bedding, sanitary items (cat litter, cleaning products), and other gently used pet care items.

  • Foster

    Open your home to an animal in need. Fostering provides temporary care, love, and a safe place for pets while they wait for their forever homes. Learn about fostering.

  • Spread the Word

    Help us reach more people by sharing our adoptable pets, events, and mission with friends, family, and your community. Every share can help an animal find a home!
