Our Story

Founded in 2026, Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a nonprofit organization built on the belief that every animal deserves a safe, loving, and permanent home. Our mission is to improve the lives of animals throughout the Twin Cities community through rescue, rehabilitation, adoption, and advocacy.

What began as a shared vision has grown into a dedicated team of experienced animal advocates, compassionate volunteers, and community supporters. Through strong local partnerships and a shared commitment to animal welfare, we work together to give animals a second chance.

Core Values

Our core values guide everything we do, from providing daily care to animals in need to building lasting relationships with adopters, volunteers, and our community. We believe every animal deserves compassion, dignity, and the opportunity for a safe and loving home.

  • Hope
    We believe every animal deserves a second chance and the opportunity for a brighter future, regardless of their past or circumstances.
  • Trust
    We build honest, dependable relationships with animals, adopters, volunteers, partners, and our community through transparency, accountability, and consistency.
  • Empathy
    We approach every animal and individual with kindness, patience, compassion, and respect.
  • Community
    We believe meaningful change happens when people work together. We encourage collaboration, education, volunteerism, and community involvement.

Partnerships

We are proud to have the support of the following organizations

  • Veterinary Clinics and Animal Hospitals
    Partner with local veterinarians for wellness exams, vaccinations, spay/neuter services, emergency care, and medical consultations.
  • Pet Supply Stores
    Coordinate donation drives, adoption events, discounted supplies, and community fundraising opportunities.
  • Local Animal Control
    Coordinate stray animal intake, lost-and-found services, animal welfare investigations, and community outreach.
  • Pet Groomers and Boarding Facilities
    Arrange discounted grooming, temporary boarding, bathing, and other care services.
  • Local Businesses and Corporate Sponsors
    Establish sponsorships, employee volunteer programs, fundraising campaigns, and in-kind donations.
  • Pet Adoption Platforms and Animal Welfare Networks
    Share adoptable animal listings and increase visibility through platforms such as Petfinder and Adopt a Pet.
  • Animal Behaviorists and Professional Trainers
    Provide behavioral assessments, training resources, rehabilitation support, and adopter education.
  • Food Banks and Community Assistance Programs
    Coordinate pet food assistance programs to help families experiencing financial hardship keep their pets.
